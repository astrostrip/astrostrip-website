    METHOD

    Deep, but readable.

    Psychological, not predictive

    astro.strip reads a chart as a pattern: what drives you, how you protect yourself, where you get in your own way. Ancient sources and myths are the evidence. The reading stays down to earth.

    Every degree counts

    Two people with a Sun in Leo are not the same. Each of the 360 degrees of the zodiac has its own meaning, and your Big Three are read at their exact degree.

    Calculated precisely

    Swiss Ephemeris, the engine professional astrology software runs on. Tropical zodiac, Placidus houses, true node, the orbs of our school. Past daylight saving times are included.

    Your data stays yours

    The chart is calculated right here in your browser. Nothing is sent, nothing is stored, no tracking, no cookies.
